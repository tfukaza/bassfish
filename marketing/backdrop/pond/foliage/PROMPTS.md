# Pond foliage sprites

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for this project. These original PNGs retain genuine transparency and are used directly by the scene. Upright plants are camera-facing billboards; the lily leaf is a horizontal textured plane. The prompts below are the exact submitted prompts.

## bank-grass

File: [bank-grass-v1.png](bank-grass-v1.png)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a single production-ready vegetation billboard sprite for a small isometric pond in a Three.js website.
Style: carefully hand-painted game environment art, natural botanical shapes, restrained fine brush texture, softly shaded volume. Earthy olive greens, fresh moss-green highlights, deep forest-green interior shadows. Soft neutral daylight from upper left. Attractive and organic, not geometric low-poly, not vector clip art, not photorealistic.
Output: PNG with a genuinely transparent alpha background, including transparent gaps between leaves. No ground plane, no soil mound, no scenery, no water, no pot, no floating cast shadow, no text, no border, no baked checkerboard or white background. Entire plant in frame. Center the subject with about 6% clear padding. Plants meet at a grounded narrow base at bottom center. Clean antialiased leaf edges without pale halos.
Subject: one graceful, asymmetrical clump of pond-bank sedge, with about 16 long slim arching blades in different lengths, narrow tapered tips, a few soft dry ochre blades near its base. Compact rather than sprawling; silhouette about as wide as tall. View the clump from slightly above, as an isolated plant in an isometric miniature. Leaves must have natural curves and convincing overlapping folds, with space between outer blades. One coherent rooted clump, not a sprite sheet.
```
