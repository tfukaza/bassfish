# Bassfish art direction

The tagline is **A local coordination layer for coding agents**. Use those exact words and capitalization.

The previous set had too much copy, repeated labels, large fish illustrations, and duplicate diagrams. This revision removes those elements. The banner and social card contain the logo, tagline, and a small note about feature status.

## Appearance

Use Helvetica Neue Regular for headlines and Medium for the wordmark. Use charcoal `#242623` on an off-white `#FAFAF8` canvas. Olive `#596650` marks workflow nodes and active states. Secondary text is `#6A6F67`; connector lines are `#D4D7D0`.

Make the combined fish and wordmark the main element of the README banner, with the tagline below in smaller type. Keep the smaller logo treatment on the social card. Do not put logos on workflow diagrams or other explanatory infographics. Logos belong in the brand reference and identity assets. The icon stays in one color and needs a 32 px square at minimum. Give static artwork at least 72 px of outer space. Avoid boxes around explanatory text.

## Content

Use a single sentence when it says enough. Describe what agents do. Remove slogans about the design, strings of short promises, repeated captions, and claims that add no information.

The workflow shows one sequence: claim, read or write, release. The diagram’s dots and arrows use one shared horizontal axis. The video uses two terminal panes for a scripted API/client exchange. Display captured Bassfish messages and revisions, highlight the active message, and dim the other pane. Keep tool arguments out of the picture; retain the full calls in the capture file.

Threads, tickets, advisory file locks, and notifications are available in version 0.4. Use “Local preview” beside the tagline in the banner and social card. The workflow covers content turns and file locks: distinguish committing content from releasing without writing, and explain that files stay in the repository. Keep exact lifetime and recovery rules in the README, where there is room to explain them.

The gallery shows one asset at a time. Downloads and the brand reference are available without putting every file on the page at once.

## References used

- [Clear Technical English by Hakan Alpay](https://hakanalpay.com/english/): give the minimum sufficient explanation and remove details that do not change understanding.
- [Taste Skill, Redesign](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/redesign-skill/SKILL.md): audit the existing work, simplify generic patterns, and fix spacing and hierarchy within the existing stack.
- [Taste Skill, Minimalist UI](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/minimalist-skill/SKILL.md): neutral colors, typographic hierarchy, and generous spacing. Decorative backgrounds, extra cards, and ambient motion were omitted to follow the request for less clutter.
