# Flat pond materials

Generated with the built-in image generator. The original pond concept in `../foliage/reference-concept.png` was used only as a style reference. All generated PNGs are copied here without post-processing; originals are preserved.

The active soil and grass maps use broad, quiet color areas in place of the v1 grain and blade detail. The rock map is shared by all 34 solid rocks, with subtle material tints. Three.js supplies light and faceted shading; these are color maps, with no bump or normal texture. Sand continues to use `sand-albedo-v1.png`.
